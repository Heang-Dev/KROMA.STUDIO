Long work, like a download, a translation, a voice-over, an export or a whole batch, can take minutes. Kroma tells you when each one finishes or fails, so you can work on something else, or in another app, in the meantime.

## The bell

The bell in the title bar, after **New**, **Open** and **Save**, keeps everything that finished or failed: downloads, subtitles, translations, voice-overs, exports, posts, stories, the voice engine and updates. A number on it counts the new ones.

Click it to open the list, newest first. Each entry has:

- a green tick (done), a red cross (failed) or an info mark,
- the title and, under it, the reason or the detail (you can select and copy it),
- how long ago it happened,
- a button when there's something to do next: **Retry**, **Open project**, **Show in Finder** (**Show in folder** on Windows and Linux), **Open Downloads**, **Open Batch**, **Open Story**, **Open Settings**, or **Restart** for an update that's ready.

**Mark all read** clears the number; **Clear** empties the list. When there's nothing, it says *You're all caught up*.

The bell keeps the last 100 notifications, and they're still there after a restart. **Retry** buttons only work until Kroma restarts; after that, start the task again from its panel.

## Three ways you're told

| Where | When |
|---|---|
| **A notice in Kroma** | At the bottom right of the window, when work finishes or fails. |
| **A system notification** | Your computer's own notification, while you're in another app. A failure shows one even while you're in Kroma. |
| **The bell** | Always. Everything goes into the bell, whatever your settings. |

Long runs don't flood you: the Download Center and Batch tell you about each video, then send one summary at the end (for example *Batch finished: 5 processed · 1 failed*).

## Settings → Notifications

Open **Settings → Notifications** to choose what you hear about.

| Option | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Notify me when Kroma is in the background** | On | System notifications. Failures show one even while you're in Kroma. |
| **Play a sound** | On | A sound when work finishes or fails, in Kroma and in system notifications. |
| **When work is done** | Glass | The sound for finished work. On a Mac, choose from the Mac's own sounds; press **Play** to hear it. |
| **When something fails** | Basso | The sound for failures. |
| **Only when something fails** | Off | Finished work then shows in the bell only, without a system notification. |
| **Send a test notification** → **Send test** | — | Shows a system notification now, and adds it to the bell. |

On Windows and Linux, system notifications use your system's own sound, and notices inside Kroma play a short chime.

### What to notify about

A switch for each kind of work: **Downloads**, **Subtitles & translation**, **Voices & stories**, **Export & posting** and **Updates**. Switched off, that kind gets no system notification and no notice in Kroma when it worked. Failures still show, and everything is still kept in the bell.

### History

**Notification history** shows how many notifications are in the bell. **Clear history** empties it.

## Not seeing them?

Your computer decides whether Kroma may show notifications, and whether they make a sound. If **Send test** shows nothing outside Kroma:

1. Open **Settings → Notifications → Not seeing them?** and press **Open**. It opens your system's notification settings.
2. On a Mac: **System Settings → Notifications → Kroma Studio**, turn on **Allow notifications** and **Play sound for notifications**. On Windows and Linux, allow notifications from Kroma Studio.
3. Press **Send test** again.

Also check that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't on, and that **Notify me when Kroma is in the background** is on.
