## 0.2 in short

0.2 is about **whole series**. Since 0.1, Kroma has grown from one video at a time into a tool for a drama with forty episodes:

- **[Download Center](downloading#the-download-center)**: paste a series, a collection, a playlist or a channel, tick the episodes, and each one becomes its own numbered project. Import a folder of episodes you already have the same way.
- **[Batch](batch)**: choose the steps once (subtitles, translation, voice-over, hiding the original subtitles, style, export, covers, post text) and every episode goes through them, one after another, then waits in **Ready to post** with the right format for each platform.
- **[Hide original subtitles](style-and-objects#hide-original-subtitles)**: Kroma finds the Chinese subtitles burned into the video and blurs exactly those.
- **[Animated captions](style-and-objects#animation)**, **33 subtitle styles**, seven Khmer display fonts, shadows and glows, and **[My styles](style-and-objects#my-styles)** for your own looks.
- **[Series glossary](translation#series-glossary)**: one spelling for every name, in every episode.
- **[Episode covers](posting#episode-covers)** with the episode number, in 16:9 and 9:16.
- **[Story studio](story)**, Kroma's own **[voice engine](voice-engine)** built in, **[notifications](notifications)** with a bell and sounds, searchable dropdowns, and more reliable updates.

Projects from 0.1 open in 0.2 and look exactly the same. Moving up needs nothing from you: see the [Upgrade guide](upgrade). The 0.1.x releases that led here are in the 0.1 docs (use the version menu at the top of the sidebar) and on the [Changelog](/changelog/).

## How versions are numbered

A Kroma version has three numbers, like **0.2.0**:

| Part | In 0.2.0 | Means |
|---|---|---|
| First | 0 | Kroma is still young. It becomes 1 at the first stable release. |
| Second | 2 | The series. These docs are for the 0.2 series. A new series can bring bigger changes, and gets its own docs. |
| Third | 0 | A release within the series: fixes and new features. Every 0.2.x release opens projects from earlier releases. |

Numbers are compared one part at a time, so 0.2.10 is newer than 0.2.9, and 0.2.0 is newer than 0.1.13.

## Which version you have

Open **Settings → About** (⌘, or Ctrl+, then **About**). The top line shows it, for example **Kroma Studio 0.2.0**. Under **Updates**, the line next to **Check for updates** also says whether you have the latest version. The version is also shown at the bottom of the Settings sidebar.

## Getting a new version

Kroma updates itself: see [Updates](updates). To install a specific version by hand, see the [Upgrade guide](upgrade). Every release, with its downloads, is also on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Heang-Dev/kroma/releases).
