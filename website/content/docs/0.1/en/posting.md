Post to YouTube, Facebook and TikTok in one click, with a title, text and hashtags written for each platform.

## Connect your accounts (once)

Kroma posts through **Upload-Post**, which the platforms have already approved, so your posts go out publicly.

1. Make a free account at [upload-post.com](https://app.upload-post.com/) and create an API key.
2. Paste it into **Settings → Posting**.
3. Press **Connect** and sign in to YouTube, Facebook and TikTok in your browser.
4. Pick your Facebook Page (Facebook only allows apps to post to Pages).

## Post a video

1. In **Post**, press **Write the posts**. The AI writes a title, text and hashtags for each platform; edit anything. They're saved with the project.
2. Tick the platforms. Choose YouTube visibility, and TikTok **Post now** or **To drafts**.
3. Press **Post**. Kroma exports (or reuses your last export if nothing changed), uploads once, and follows each platform to the end.

**Posted** keeps a history with a link to each post. Facebook gets a Reel for vertical videos up to 90 seconds, otherwise a Page video.

## Plans

YouTube and Facebook work on Upload-Post's free plan (10 posts a month). TikTok needs their Basic plan.
