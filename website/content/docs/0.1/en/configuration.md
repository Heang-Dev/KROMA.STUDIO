Kroma uses AI services with **your own keys**: you pay Kroma nothing, and only what a service charges, which for most is nothing to start. Keys are encrypted with your system's keychain and only sent to the service they belong to.

The first time you open Kroma, it opens **Settings → Translation**.

## Translation and posts

Choose a translator in **Settings → Translation**:

| Translator | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gemini | Free tier | The default. Get a key at [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey). Also writes your social posts. |
| Google Translate | Free, no key | Basic wording. Good when you have no key. |
| Claude | Paid | The most natural Khmer, best with names and tone. |

## Speech to text

In **Settings → Speech to Text**, add a free [Groq key](https://console.groq.com/keys). Groq runs Whisper large-v3 and transcribes a whole movie in seconds. Gemini (with your Gemini key) and OpenAI work too.

## Downloads

Some sites only give high quality, or members-only videos, when you're signed in. In **Settings → Downloads**, choose the browser you're signed in with; Kroma uses its sign-in for downloads.

## Folders

**Settings → Folders** sets where exports and downloads go. By default: your Videos (or Movies) folder → Kroma Studio.
