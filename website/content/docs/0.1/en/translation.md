Kroma translates the meaning the way people talk, with the right politeness, common spellings for foreign words, and each line kept short enough to read and to say in the time it's on screen.

## Notes for the translator

In **Translate → Notes for the translator**, write names, who's who and the tone, e.g. “Palace drama. 李明 is the hero, write it លី មីង. The emperor speaks formally.” Gemini and Claude follow them.

## Fixing words everywhere

The **Find & replace** button in the Subtitles list changes a word in every line at once. Tick **Remember for future translations** to add the fix to your notes, so the next translation spells it right.

Kroma also checks every translated line: a line with letters from another script (a Thai or Chinese word inside Khmer) is translated again automatically.

## Other languages

In **Translate → Translate to**, choose Khmer, Thai, Vietnamese, Lao, English, Indonesian or Malay. The social posts and the instant voices follow the language; the 36 designed voices are made for Khmer.

## When Gemini is busy

Free Gemini keys have a daily limit. When it's reached, Kroma translates the remaining lines with Google Translate and tells you. Translate again later for Gemini's wording.
