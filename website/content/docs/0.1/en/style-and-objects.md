## Subtitle style

In **Style**, set the font size, colours, background, outline and position. **Bilingual** shows the original line above the Khmer one. The preview shows exactly what the export will look like.

## Covering original subtitles

Many videos have subtitles burned into the picture. In **Objects**, **Cover original subtitles** blurs them in one click.

## Objects

Also in **Objects**:

- **Blur area**: hide anything (a watermark, a face, a number plate).
- **Image / logo**: your logo or a sponsor's.
- **Text**: a caption or a call to action.

Drag an object on the preview to move it, and its corner to resize it. Each can show for the whole video, or from one time to another.

## Formats

Choose **16:9** (YouTube), **9:16** (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) or **1:1** at the top. A video that doesn't fit gets blurred or black bars, or is filled.
