A voice-over reads the translated lines aloud, timed to each line. In a drama, every character can have their own voice.

## Characters

In **Voice → Characters**:

1. **Find automatically** reads the dialogue (about 20 seconds), names each character and suggests a voice. Or press **Add a character**.
2. Pick a voice for each character. ▶ plays a sample.
3. To change who says a line, click the coloured name on that line in the Subtitles list.

Lines without a character use the **Default voice**.

## Voices

| Kind | What | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Designed | 36 Khmer voices for every kind of character: narration, palace & royalty, heroes, modern drama, family, children, villains | The [voice engine](voice-engine) |
| Cloned | Your own voice, from a short recording | The [voice engine](voice-engine) |
| Instant | Microsoft's free voices, every language | Nothing |

Hear them all in **Voices** (left menu).

## Make the voice-over

Press **Make voice-over**. Each character's lines are spoken in their voice; changing one character's voice re-voices only their lines.

The **original voices are removed automatically** (music and effects stay), so no original speech is heard under the voice-over. Switch that off in **Voice → Mix**. It needs [uv](installation).
