## Upgrading to 0.1.1 from 0.1.0

0.1.0 can't update itself. Download 0.1.1 or later once from the [download page](/#download) and install it over the old one; your settings, keys and projects stay. From 0.1.1 on, updates come by themselves ([Updates](updates)).

## Upgrading within 0.1

Every 0.1.x version updates automatically, and projects open in any later 0.1.x version. Nothing to do.
