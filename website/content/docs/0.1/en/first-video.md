The **step bar** along the bottom of the editor shows what's done and what's next: Video → Subtitles → Khmer → Characters → Voices → Style → Export.

## 1. Add a video

In **Media**, paste a link and press **Get**. It works with YouTube, Bilibili, Douyin, Youku, iQiyi, Facebook, TikTok and over 1,000 more sites, even a whole share message (Kroma reads the title from it). Or press **Import** to use a file.

## 2. Make the subtitles

In **Translate**, check the spoken language and press **Make Khmer subtitles**. Kroma uses the site's own subtitles when it has them, otherwise speech-to-text, then translates.

## 3. Check the lines

Fix anything by typing in the **Subtitles** list. Click a line's time to jump there. See [Translation](translation) for notes, find & replace and other languages.

## 4. Voices (optional)

Give the video a voice-over, with a different voice for each character: see [Characters & voices](characters-and-voices).

## 5. Style and export

Style the subtitles and cover the original ones ([Style & objects](style-and-objects)), then press **Export** at the top right ([Export](export)).

Your project saves itself as you work and reopens when you start Kroma.
